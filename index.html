About

I have always been drawn to the patterns hidden inside complex systems.

I am a data scientist with more than five years of experience across predictive modeling, anomaly detection, NLP, large-scale data pipelines, and production machine learning.

My work has taken me from hardware reliability to financial risk, but the question has always been the same—how can data help us understand complex systems a little earlier and a little better? Outside work, I have followed smaller questions through computer vision, language, education, web systems, and data engineering.
